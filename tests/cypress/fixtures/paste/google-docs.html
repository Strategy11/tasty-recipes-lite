Weeknight Turkey Chili

A hearty, one-pot chili that comes together in under an hour.

Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cook Time: 40 minutes

Serves 6

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground turkey

  • 1 onion, diced

  • 2 cans kidney beans, drained

For the topping

  • 1 cup shredded cheddar

Directions

  1. Brown the turkey and onion in a large pot.

  2. Add the beans and simmer for 30 minutes.

Why you'll love it

It is cozy and freezes well.

Storage

Freeze for up to 3 months.
