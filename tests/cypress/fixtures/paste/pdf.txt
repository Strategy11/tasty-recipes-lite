Lemon Ricotta Pancakes

Fluffy pancakes with a bright lemon flavor that
everyone loves on a lazy weekend.

Prep Time: 10 mins
Cook Time: 15 mins
Yield: 8 pancakes

INGREDIENTS
1 cup all-purpose flour
2 tbsp sugar
1 cup whole milk ricotta
2 eggs

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Whisk the flour and sugar together in a
large bowl.
2. Fold in the ricotta and eggs until just
combined.
3. Cook on a hot griddle until golden.
2

NOTES
Leftovers keep in the fridge for 2 days.

Nutrition
Calories: 210
Protein: 9g
