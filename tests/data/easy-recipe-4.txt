<div class="ERHead">Prep time: <time itemprop="prepTime" datetime="PT12M">12 mins</time></div>

<div class="ERSummary summary">Suitable accompaniment's to this risotto-like dish are crusty bread, an avocado and orange salad with a cilantro vinaigrette and, to top it off, coconut pie.</div>
<div class="ERIngredients">
<div class="ERIngredientsHeader">Ingredients</div>
<ul class="ingredients">
 	<li class="ingredient">2 1/2 cups (about) canned vegetable broth</li>
 	<li class="ingredient">1 15- to 16-ounce can kidney beans, drained</li>
 	<li class="ingredient">1 cup canned unsweetened regular or light coconut milk</li>
 	<li class="ingredient">1 tablespoon minced seeded jalapeño chili</li>
 	<li class="ingredient">1 teaspoon dried thyme</li>
 	<li class="ingredient">1/4 teaspoon ground allspice</li>
 	<li class="ingredient">3/4 cup medium-grain white rice</li>
 	<li class="ingredient">1 cup thinly sliced green onions</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="ERInstructions">
<div class="ERInstructionsHeader">Instructions</div>
<div class="instructions">
<ol>
 	<li class="instruction">Combine 2 cups vegetable broth, kidney beans, coconut milk, minced jalapño chili, thyme and allspice in heavy large saucepan. Bring mixture to boil over medium-high heat. Stir in rice. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer mixture uncovered until most of liquid is absorbed and rice is almost tender, stirring often, about 20 minutes.</li>
 	<li class="instruction">Mix 3/4 cup green onions into rice. Continue to simmer until rice is very tender and mixture is creamy, adding more broth by 1/4 cupfuls if mixture seems dry, about 5 minutes longer. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Transfer to serving bowl. Sprinkle with remaining 1/4 cup green onions and serve.</li>
</ol>
</div>
