One of our Favorite Summer Recipes is this <strong>No-Bake Strawberry Lemon Cheesecake Recipe</strong>. This only requires a few simple ingredients and all you need to do is chill overnight in the fridge so you don't have to worry about heating up your oven on a hot summer day!

My favorite way to make this recipe is in the Mini Graham Cracker Crusts you can buy at the store, but you can also make a larger more traditional size cheesecake if you wish.
<p style="text-align: center;"><img class="aligncenter  wp-image-500128" alt="1708" src="http://www.passionforsavings.com/content/uploads/2013/05/1708.jpg" width="371" height="251" /></p>
<p style="text-align: left;">All you need for this recipe are the following: Cream Cheese, Sweetened Condensed Milk, Vanilla and Lemon Juice. Then just add in the Pie Crusts and Fresh Strawberries and you're good to go!</p>

<div class="item ERName">No-Bake Strawberry Lemon Cheesecake Recipe</div>
<div class="ERClear"></div>
<div class="ERIngredients">
<div class="ERIngredientsHeader">Ingredients</div>
<ul class="ingredients">
	<li class="ingredient">1 8 oz. package of Cream Cheese</li>
	<li class="ingredient">1 Can of Sweetened Condensed Milk</li>
	<li class="ingredient">1/3 Cup Lemon Juice</li>
	<li class="ingredient">1 tsp. Vanilla</li>
	<li class="ingredient">Graham Cracker Crusts</li>
	<li class="ingredient">Fresh Strawberries for Topping</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="ERInstructions">
<div class="ERInstructionsHeader">Instructions</div>
<div class="instructions">
<ol>
	<li class="instruction">Combine Softened Cream Cheese, Sweetened Condensed Milk and Vanilla in a Mixer and Mix Well.</li>
	<li class="instruction">Slowly add in Lemon Juice and Mix</li>
	<li class="instruction">Pour into Graham Cracker Crusts and Chill Overnight</li>
	<li class="instruction">Top with Fresh Strawberries</li>
</ol>
</div>
